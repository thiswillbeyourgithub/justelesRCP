- Usable by AI assistants: give justelesrcp.olicorne.org/llms.txt once to Claude, ChatGPT, Le Chat or Gemini and ask it to remember the site. It can then find any drug and quote its official RCP, section by section, instead of answering from memory. [019ee27]
